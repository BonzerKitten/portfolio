About me

Hello there! My name is Mateus Ferreira, and I started my career as a gameplay programmer for console games.

For about eight years, I've worked closely with companies such as Sony, Microsoft, and Nintendo.

Nowadays, I enjoy drinking a cold cup of cashew juice while making all sorts of cool effects for an unannounced project at Wargaming UK.
